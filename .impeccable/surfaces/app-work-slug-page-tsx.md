# Selected Work Detail Surface Brief

- **Scope and mode:** `app/work/[slug]/page.tsx` is a reusable Experience/Read case-study surface. A visitor arrives from the homepage to understand why a real product needed to exist, how Jiaming reasoned about it, what he owned, and what defensible change followed. VivoFlow is the canonical content-rich sample; Huawei and Kupo World may vary their evidence without changing the page family.
- **Opening and sequence:** Begin with a quiet `Selected Work › Project` return path, project title, one-sentence problem or mechanism, and compact company / role / date metadata. Lead with the problem and product mechanism rather than metrics or architecture, then alternate concise narrative chapters with meaningful visuals. No navbar, hero CTA, cover treatment, sticky table of contents, resume framing, or technology-tag wall.
- **Visual proof:** Inherit the homepage paper-and-type language while allowing wider figures and richer pacing. Prose remains a narrow reading column; selected figures expand, sit directly on the paper field, and carry short captions without cards. VivoFlow uses three to four explicitly conceptual, public-safe illustrations for fragmented activity, the shared traceable workflow, rollout, human-in-the-loop document review, and end-to-end ownership. They must not resemble real UI or disclose internal architecture.
- **Evidence variants and boundaries:** Huawei uses public-safe conceptual CI-to-hardware-analytics-to-version-planning visuals without screenshots or internal identifiers. Kupo World uses retrieved original assets and a clearly qualified comparison with the later official feature; do not reconstruct missing assets or imply unverified attribution. Never invent claims, metrics, regulatory outcomes, screenshots, feedback learning, or confidential system detail. `300+` means organizational rollout reach, not active users.
- **Wayfinding and behavior:** End every page with a deterministic `← Back to Selected Work` link to the homepage section and an optional `Next project →` link. Links and figures remain keyboard accessible; motion is restrained and reduced-motion safe. Mobile preserves the story order, collapses prose and figures to one column, and keeps captions attached to their figures.
