## What was approved

- Paper-like low-contrast visual language and typography.
- Problem-and-product-mechanism opening.
- Alternating narrow narrative text with wider conceptual illustrations.
- Direct-on-paper figures with restrained captions and no card containers.
- Overall chapter rhythm, whitespace, and Selected Work page character.

## Required corrections before implementation

- Replace the bottom-left `Explore more Selected Work` treatment with a deterministic `← Back to Selected Work` link to the homepage section.
- Remove the generated `Feedback loop to improve future reviews`; human objections are retained as traceable records but are not automatically used to train or improve the Agent.
- Express `300+` only as company-wide organizational rollout reach, never as active-user count.
- Treat all diagrams as conceptual public-safe explanations, never as the production UI or internal architecture.
