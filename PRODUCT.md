## Platform

web

## Users

The site is a public personal profile for anyone who wants to understand Jiaming Zhang. Visitors may include peers, collaborators, friends, prospective clients, and hiring teams, but the product does not optimize its voice or structure around a single conversion audience.

## Product Purpose

The site is Jiaming Zhang's long-term digital calling card. It helps visitors understand who he is, what he has built, how he approaches work and decisions, and what he chooses to share over time.

Success means the page feels like a coherent portrait of a person through both work and thought. It must not read as an `Open to Work` landing page, a client-acquisition funnel, or a résumé reproduced on the web.

## Positioning

The content centers on work and thinking together. Real projects and outcomes establish engineering credibility; methods, judgment, and future writing reveal the person behind that work.

Jiaming Zhang's professional identity is Full-stack & Agent Engineer. His strongest evidence is end-to-end ownership of reliable systems and human-in-the-loop Agents for complex, real-world workflows, including work in highly regulated healthcare.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve keyboard interaction, native dialog focus behavior, reduced-motion support, responsive readability, and working external links.
- Do not fabricate projects, metrics, endorsements, regulatory outcomes, screenshots, or causal claims.
- Do not expose private company systems, internal platform names, production screenshots, real company data, repository names, hardware identifiers, or internal architecture.
- VivoFlow and Huawei case-study visuals may use purpose-built conceptual diagrams based only on confirmed public-safe facts.
- Hide empty sections instead of publishing placeholders. Existing third-party `Approach` copy may remain only while the site is WIP and must be replaced before public launch.
- Contact language should welcome general connection without signaling job-search status.

## Brand Commitments

- The page should feel like reading a tactile personal document rather than browsing a conventional portfolio template.
- The background theme color is the warm paper-like beige `rgb(247, 240, 231)` (`#F7F0E7`).
- Body text must use a slightly softened dark gray rather than pure black, preserving strong readability against the beige ground.
- The overall identity should carry a hand-drawn quality without sacrificing reading comfort.
- Hierarchy should come primarily from typography, whitespace, and restrained width changes rather than cards, repeated frames, or ornamental section chrome.
- Typography uses distinct roles: a confident modern sans for display and structure, a literary serif for sustained reading, and sparse handwriting only for authored annotations. Bold, italic, and slightly irregular underlines may emphasize meaning without becoming decoration.
- Motion should remain quiet and purposeful: short responses on links, underlines, and occasional illustrations, with no large scroll-driven transitions and full reduced-motion support.
- Avoid literal scrapbook devices such as tape, torn edges, sticky notes, simulated paper fibers, or pervasive doodles.
- A future line-art cartoon logo will be generated from Jiaming Zhang's real likeness. The current Filip `F` favicon must not ship.
- The browser title is only `Jiaming Zhang`.
- The metadata description is `Full-stack and Agent Engineer building reliable systems for complex, real-world workflows.`

## Product Principles

1. Present a person, not a conversion funnel.
2. Let real work and thoughtful reflection reinforce each other.
3. Prefer precise, defensible evidence over inflated claims.
4. Protect confidential work even when disclosure would make a case study easier.
5. Publish only complete, personally true content; keep unfinished sections out of the public experience.
